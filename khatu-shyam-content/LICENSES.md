# Licenses for this content pack

## Wikimedia Commons assets

Files with `source: "wikimedia"` in `metadata.json` are from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY, CC BY-SA, or CC0).

For each such file you must:

1. Keep the license intact.
2. Credit the author (see `attribution` and `sourcePage` in `metadata.json`).
3. For **CC BY-SA**: if you modify the image, ShareAlike may apply to your adaptation — prefer distributing the resized phone crop with attribution, or replace with originals you own.

Do not strip EXIF/credit when redistributing unless your lawyer confirms it is OK for your use case; the catalog already stores attribution in `metadata.json`.

## Original / app-owned assets

Files with `source: "generated"` were created for this project:

- Gradient quote wallpapers (Pillow)
- Synthesized ringtone tones (ffmpeg / PCM)

These are placeholders you can ship; replace with higher-quality commissioned art and licensed audio when ready.

## Not included (intentionally)

- Copyrighted YouTube / Spotify bhajans
- Random Google Images wallpaper packs
- Unlicensed temple murti close-ups from blogs
